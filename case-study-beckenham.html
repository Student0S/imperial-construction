Project Anatomy

Crafting penthouse comfort under Edwardian rafters.

1. The Challenge

The homeowners in Beckenham had a growing family and urgently required a quiet, private sanctuary away from the busy ground and first floors. Their existing roof pitch was shallow (32 degrees) with awkward cross-timber struts and cold spots throughout winter.

The key objective was maximising usable standing height along the perimeter while ensuring full acoustic privacy from the bedrooms below.

2. Engineering & Design Solution

Imperial designed a bespoke flat-roof rear dormer engineered with multi-layer acoustic floor insulation (dense mineral wool and resilient bars) and Kingspan high-performance PIR rigid board across the entire roof envelope.

Highlights of the transformation:

  • Balcony: Full-height double glazed French doors opening onto an architectural glass Juliet balcony overlooking the rear gardens.
  • Natural Daylight: Dual electronically controlled Velux roof windows with integrated rain sensors above the bed and stairwell.
  • Wetroom Suite: Frameless walk-in glass shower, wall-mounted brushed bronze thermostatic brassware, and continuous micro-cement finishes.
  • Custom Joinery: Hand-built eve storage drawers and a walk-in wardrobe with soft-close integrated warm LED lighting.

3. The Handover & Results

44 square metres of luxury bedroom, dressing room, and en-suite space handed over in 8 weeks. Completed fully under Permitted Development with Certificate of Lawfulness issued by Bromley council.
