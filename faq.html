Planning Permission & Permitted Development

Do I need full planning permission for my rear extension or loft conversion?
Not necessarily. Many single-storey rear extensions up to 3m (or up to 6m/8m under Prior Approval Large Home Extension rules) and loft conversions up to 40 cubic metres (terraced) or 50 cubic metres (semi-detached/detached) fall under Permitted Development Rights. However, if your home is in a Conservation Area (such as parts of Chislehurst, Bickley, or Dulwich) or is a listed building or flat, full planning permission is required. We carry out this assessment during our first consultation and manage Certificate of Lawful Development submissions.
How long does local council approval take across Bromley and Southwark?
Standard planning decisions take 8 weeks from validation by the local authority. Lawful Development Certificates generally take 6 to 8 weeks. While planning determination is in progress, we conduct structural engineering calculations, drainage CCTV surveys, and party wall negotiations so that groundwork commences immediately upon approval.
Who handles Building Control inspections and sign-offs?
Imperial manages all Building Control notifications and site visits from start to finish. We work with approved private inspectors or Bromley/local council officers at every key stage: excavations, steel beam installation, thermal insulation, damp-proofing, and final completion. You receive the official Building Regulations Completion Certificate directly.

Party Wall Notices & Structural Calculations

When do I need to serve a Party Wall Notice on my neighbours?
Under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996, you must give formal notice if you are cutting into or inserting steel beams into a shared wall (loft conversions), building up to or astride the boundary line, or excavating foundations within 3 metres of a neighbouring structure below their foundation depth. Notices should be served at least 1 to 2 months prior to works starting. We provide templates and recommend trusted party wall surveyors when required.
Can Imperial arrange structural engineering and load-bearing calculations?
Yes. We collaborate with chartered structural engineers who produce all required beam sizing, goalpost portal frame designs, and padstone specifications. These calculations are submitted to Building Control for statutory sign-off before fabrication and installation.

Milestone Invoicing & Budget Certainty

Do you demand large upfront deposits before starting?
No. We operate on a fair, risk-mitigated milestone invoicing schedule. An initial 10% mobilization deposit locks in your project start date and funds procurement of specialized long-lead items. Subsequent payments are strictly linked to verified physical completion (e.g. Groundworks complete, Weathertight shell erected, First Fix inspected). You only pay for inspected, tangible progress.
How are variations or changes during the build handled?
Every variation must be agreed upon in writing before any work is undertaken. We provide an itemised breakdown showing exact material and labour adjustments, ensuring there are zero unexpected surprises or hidden extras on your final account.
Is there a retention fee held until the snagging list is complete?
Yes. A final 5% milestone retention is retained until you and Musa have walked through the finished property, completed every minor snagging item, and verified all Building Control certificates and warranties.

Living On-Site & Daily Cleanliness

Can we remain living in our home during a loft conversion or rear extension?
Yes, for the vast majority of our projects. For loft conversions, all heavy structural works, timber cuts, and steel crane lifts are accessed externally via scaffolding through the roof, keeping internal household disruption minimal until the staircase is installed. For extensions, temporary dust screens and secure partitions seal off the rest of your home, and we preserve essential services (water, heating, cooking) throughout.
What are your working hours and site protocols?
Our standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 08:00 to 17:00, strictly respecting local council noise regulations. Heavy noisy demolition or drilling is confined to agreed midday windows. At the end of each working day, all walkways are swept, tools secured, and dust protection maintained.

Insurance, Guarantees & Credentials

What insurance cover does Imperial hold?
Imperial Real Estate Holdings Ltd holds comprehensive £10,000,000 Public Liability Insurance and £10,000,000 Employers' Liability Insurance. Copies of our current policy schedules and insurance certificates are provided directly within your initial tender pack.
What warranties are provided upon project completion?
We provide a comprehensive written 12 to 24-month craftsmanship warranty covering all finishes, plumbing, and electrical installations, alongside an extended 10-year structural guarantee on new roofs, timber frames, and foundations. In addition, manufacturer warranties (e.g. 25-year rubber/fibreglass roofing membranes, Schluter wetroom membranes, boiler warranties) are transferred directly into your handover dossier.
