Project Anatomy

Crafted around the culinary arts and everyday ergonomics.

1. The Challenge

The homeowners, avid home chefs and regular entertainers, possessed an outdated builder-spec kitchen installed over a decade ago. Cramped laminate countertops, limited refrigeration capacity, and cumbersome overhead extraction hood vents obstructed eye contact with dinner guests.

Furthermore, because the family needed to remain in residence throughout the refurbishment, the works required a meticulous clean-site protocol with daily dust extraction, zip-wall airlocks, and strict working hours.

2. Engineering & Design Solution

Imperial's specialist interior team redesigned the layout around an oversized 3.4-metre central island with double mitred waterfall ends:

  • Island & Surfaces: Calacatta Gold engineered quartz with seamless vein-matching across worktop, waterfall gable ends, and rear splashback.
  • Extraction & Cooking: Integrated Bora Pure downdraft induction hob recessed flush into the island, eliminating overhead visual clutter.
  • Concealed Breakfast Nook: Retractable pocket-doors revealing an internal walnut bar with integrated LED sensor illumination, power docks, and espresso machine station.
  • Cabinetry & Joinery: Solid tulipwood framing with hand-sprayed polyurethane lacquer in deep heritage forest green, accented by unlacquered solid brass knobs.
  • Sub-Floor Preparation: Self-levelling compound application with acoustic decoupling matting before laying engineered European parquet.

3. The Handover & Results

Completed in exactly 5 weeks from demolition to final silicone polish. The clients were provided with complete NICEIC electrical sign-off certificates for all new high-load appliance rings and an insurance-backed workmanship warranty.
