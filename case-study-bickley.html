Project Anatomy

Straightening complex roof geometry to unlock dual independent rooms.

1. The Challenge

An end-of-terrace 1930s home in Bickley featured a severely sloping side hip roof. A standard flat-roof rear dormer alone would not yield enough headroom to create the two distinct spaces the clients required: a high-spec executive home office for uninterrupted international calls, and a spacious double guest suite.

Moreover, sound transfer between the home office and the family bedrooms directly beneath was a critical concern requiring specialist acoustic isolation.

2. Engineering & Design Solution

Imperial executed a hip-to-gable conversion, building up the sloping side hip in matching London stock brickwork to create a vertical gable wall, paired with a substantial flat-roof rear dormer:

  • Structural Timber & Steel: Installed paired structural ridge steels and upgraded ceiling floor joists with acoustic resilient bars and Rockwool RW3 dense acoustic mineral slab insulation (54dB sound attenuation).
  • Dual-Zone Layout: Architecturally partitioned into an executive study with twin solar-control Velux rooflights and Cat6 hardwired data outlets, plus a double bedroom with built-in push-to-open eaves joinery.
  • Bespoke Staircase: Custom-built timber flight with matching continuous handrail and painted spindles, perfectly mirroring the home’s original 1930s ground-to-first floor balustrade.
  • Thermal Performance: 150mm rigid PIR insulation throughout dormer cheeks and flat roof, exceeding current Part L Building Regulation thermal U-values.

3. The Handover & Results

48 sq m of prime floor area created under General Permitted Development (Certificate of Lawfulness secured). Completed in 9 weeks with zero disturbance to the first-floor bedrooms below, complete Building Control completion certificate handed over, and backed by a 24-month structural warranty.
