Bespoke Construction & Renovation

Our Disciplines & Services

From conceptual feasibility through architectural delivery, Imperial provides transparent, end-to-end building services with fixed-price commitments and dedicated project oversight.

01 / Architectural Roof Space

Bespoke Loft Conversions

Transform dead attic space into high-value master suites, children’s rooms, or peaceful penthouse offices. Whether under Permitted Development or full planning permission, we handle all aspects from party wall notices to structural calculations and building control approval.

  • Dormer Conversions: Maximum headroom, vertical walls, and French or Juliet balcony doors.
  • Hip-to-Gable: Straightens sloping hipped roofs on end-of-terrace or semi-detached homes for dramatic floor space.
  • Mansard Conversions: Elegant 72-degree rear elevations favoured in conservation areas.
  • Complete Interior Fit-out: Integrated wardrobes, skylights, acoustic insulation, and luxury en-suites.

02 / Expanding Living Horizons

Single, Double & Wraparound Extensions

Reinvent how you experience family life. We specialise in rear and side-return extensions that dissolve the boundary between indoors and garden, flooding your ground floor with natural light through architectural glazing and structural rooflights.

  • Rear & Side-Return: Perfect for Victorian and Edwardian properties to create large open-plan zones.
  • Double-Storey Additions: Add ground floor reception spaces and first floor bedrooms simultaneously.
  • Structural Steelwork: Seamless flush ceilings without intrusive dropped downstands.
  • Climate & Comfort: High-efficiency hydronic underfloor heating, solar glass, and smart zoning.

03 / Whole Home Transformation

Full House Renovations & Restoration

For period acquisitions or complete modernization. We strip back properties to the brickwork, rectify historic deficiencies, and reconstruct them with superior thermal efficiency, pristine plasterwork, and modern technology.

  • M&E Infrastructure: Complete rewiring (NICEIC certified) and upgraded unvented plumbing.
  • Period Restoration: Sash window refurbishment, heritage cornicing, and authentic woodwork.
  • Turnkey Project Management: Single point of contact, daily logs, and staged milestone payments.
  • Peace of Mind: Covered by £10M Public Liability and comprehensive written build warranties.
