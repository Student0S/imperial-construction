Common Questions

Frequently asked about our process.

How quickly do we start after the initial enquiry?

We respond to all enquiries within 24 hours. A site visit is typically booked within 3 to 5 working days, and a detailed schedule of works follows within a week. Lead times for project commencement depend on the scope and planning status, usually 4 to 8 weeks.

Do I need finished architectural drawings before contacting you?

Not at all. If you have drawings, great—we can price from them immediately. If not, we can introduce trusted architectural partners and guide you through Permitted Development or full planning permission.

What happens if we want to change something mid-build?

Changes happen. Any variation is priced upfront in a clear written addendum with your approval before any extra work proceeds. No surprise charges on your invoice.

How do the staged invoice payments work?

We break down the total cost into 4 to 6 milestones (e.g., Foundations & Steel, Shell Watertight, First-Fix Electrics & Plumbing, Plastering, Final Handover). You inspect the stage with your site manager, and only then is that stage invoiced.
