Got Questions?

Process & pricing FAQ.

How quickly can you start after planning approval?

We typically schedule builds 4 to 8 weeks in advance to allow for structural steel fabrication and party wall notifications. If your planning drawings and party wall agreements are already complete, we can often mobilize within 3 to 4 weeks.

Do we need to move out during an extension or loft conversion?

For most loft conversions and ground-floor extensions, families comfortably remain in their home. We install temporary floor-to-ceiling zip-dust walls, seal the work zone, and ensure you always have running hot water and functioning facilities.

What happens if we want to change something mid-build?

Variations are common as spaces come to life. Any change is priced clearly in writing with an updated schedule before work commences, ensuring you retain 100% control over the final cost.

Who handles Local Authority Building Control inspections?

We manage all Building Control notices and schedule the building inspector for foundations, structural steel, insulation, and the final completion certificate on your behalf.
