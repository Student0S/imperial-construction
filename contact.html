Client Guidance

Frequently asked questions.

What geographical locations do you cover?

Imperial is based in Bromley and primarily serves residential clients across the London Borough of Bromley (including Chislehurst, Beckenham, Bickley, Orpington), South London (Dulwich, Blackheath), and parts of West Kent.

Do you manage building control and structural calculations?

Yes. We can manage the complete architectural journey from building control notifications, party wall notices, structural steel calculations, and final local authority completion certificates.

How do milestone payments work during the build?

We never ask for large upfront lump sums. Payments are structured in clearly defined stages linked directly to verifiable physical progress on site (e.g. foundations & steelwork, structural watertight shell, first-fix M&E, plastering, and final handover).

What guarantees are provided upon project completion?

All our structural works and conversions include a formal 12 to 24-month warranty against defects, supported by our full £10M Public Liability insurance policy.
