Project Anatomy

Eliminating grout lines and leak risks with engineered micro-cement.

1. The Challenge

The existing bathroom suffered from historic moisture issues common to older detached houses: a compromised shower-over-bath arrangement, decayed subfloor timber framing beneath the bath waste, and stained cement tile grout susceptible to constant mould buildup.

The clients sought an ultra-minimalist, hotel-grade level-access wetroom that required virtually zero maintenance and had complete, verifiable waterproofing guarantees.

2. Engineering & Design Solution

Imperial's specialist wetroom fit-out team stripped the bathroom to the bare structural joists and implemented an uncompromising technical specification:

  • Structural Joist Sistering: Replaced damp timbers and sistered existing joists with structural C24 timber, creating an immovable, deflection-free subfloor necessary for permanent waterproofing integrity.
  • Schluter Waterproof Tanking: Installed a full Schluter-KERDI tanking membrane with floor-level stainless steel linear drainage, hot-welded corner seals, and a 100% waterproof guarantee.
  • Seamless Micro-Cement: Multi-coat artisan micro-cement applied over walls and floor in warm taupe-grey, sealed with polyurethane matte sealer. Eliminating tile grout lines entirely for hygienic, mould-free maintenance.
  • Concealed Thermostatic Brassware: Recessed brushed bronze thermostatic shower controls with oversized rainhead, accompanied by bespoke backlit recessed shelving niches.
  • Under-Tile Electric Heating: Programmable touchscreen smart thermostatic floor heating ensuring rapid moisture evaporation and warm barefoot comfort.

3. The Handover & Results

Delivered in just 3 weeks with meticulous protection of the client's stairs and carpets. Handed over with a 10-year waterproofing guarantee and complete Part P electrical certification for the ambient LED lighting and demister mirror.
