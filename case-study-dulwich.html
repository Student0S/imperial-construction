Project Anatomy

Restoring century-old architectural character while integrating 21st-century comfort.

1. The Challenge

This grand Edwardian family home had suffered from decades of neglect and poorly executed alterations: dangerous rubber-clad electrical wiring dating back to the 1970s, leaky lead pipework, zero insulation in the exterior solid brick walls, and bowed ceiling joists caused by unsupported chimney breasts above.

The homeowners requested a complete structural strip-back to rectify all latent structural defects while preserving and restoring the original decorative plaster mouldings, timber sash frames, and grand high ceilings.

2. Engineering & Restoration Solution

Imperial mobilised a dedicated multi-disciplinary crew under director Musa Abdul:

  • Structural Interventions: Safely dismantled two unstable chimney breasts through three floors, installing permanent structural steel gallows brackets and flitch lintels signed off by Bromley and Southwark Building Control.
  • Thermal Envelope: Applied internal breathable wood-fibre insulation boards paired with traditional architectural lime plaster, eliminating thermal bridges and damp without suffocating the historic brickwork.
  • Complete MEP Overhaul: 100% NICEIC rewiring with discreet architectural bronze dimmer switches, full unvented Megaflo hot water cylinder system delivering equal pressure across four bathrooms, and CAT6 data networking throughout.
  • Heritage Joinery: Master craftsmen carefully reproduced damaged ceiling roses, reinstated period picture rails, and refurbished every original box sash window with concealed draught-proofing and slimline double glazing.

3. The Handover & Results

Over 280 sq m of magnificent residential space was handed over in 22 weeks. The building’s Energy Performance Certificate jumped from an inefficient 'F' to a warm, energy-efficient 'C', saving thousands annually in running costs while providing an enduring family home.
