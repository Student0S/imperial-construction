Extension & Kitchen
Bromley · Chislehurst

Victorian Villa Wraparound Extension

The Challenge

The family of five had outgrown their Victorian semi but didn't want to move. The original ground floor was compartmentalised into separate rooms with a galley kitchen cut off from the garden. Planning constraints on the side return made a full wraparound complex, but achievable with careful design.

Our Solution

Engineered a structural steel frame spanning 8 metres to eliminate the load-bearing wall, creating a single open-plan kitchen, dining, and living zone. Installed ultra-slim Crittall-style aluminium doors with floor-to-ceiling glazing, and integrated a flush patio threshold for seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Underfloor heating throughout, bespoke shaker cabinetry, and a central island with waterfall quartz worktops.

The Result

65 sq m of usable space added. The family gained a kitchen that's now the heart of the home, with direct garden access. Completed in 14 weeks, on budget, with full Building Control sign-off and a 12-month structural warranty.

Loft Conversion
Bromley · Beckenham

Dormer Master Suite & Dressing Room

The Challenge

The homeowners needed a master suite but their Edwardian semi's attic was uninhabitable — a cold, uninsulated space with a sloping roof pitch that limited standing height to under 2 metres at the edges. Permitted Development rules would allow a rear dormer, but the design needed to avoid overlooking neighbours.

Our Solution

Constructed a full rear dormer with a Juliet balcony to maximise headroom and natural light. Added acoustic ceiling insulation to isolate the suite from family noise below. Installed a fitted walk-in wardrobe with integrated lighting, a boutique en-suite with frameless walk-in shower and freestanding bath, and a concealed Velux rooflight above the landing stairwell for daylight penetration.

The Result

44 sq m of habitable space added without planning permission (under Permitted Development). The master suite now includes a walk-in wardrobe, en-suite, and private balcony. Completed in 8 weeks with full Building Control sign-off.

Full Renovation
South London · Dulwich

Edwardian Residence Whole-Home Refurbishment

The Challenge

A four-storey Edwardian family home in Dulwich needed complete modernisation. Original features were buried under decades of unsympathetic renovation — degraded plasterwork, outdated wiring from the 1970s, no central heating on the upper floors, and a kitchen that hadn't been touched since the 1980s. The clients wanted period character restored, but with contemporary thermal efficiency.

Our Solution

Stripped the property back to brick. Removed two structural chimney breasts with full steelwork support, installed a complete new thermal insulation envelope (internal wall insulation with breathable lime plaster to preserve the historic fabric), full NICEIC-certified rewiring, and upgraded to an unvented pressurised plumbing system with underfloor heating on the ground floor. Restored original cornicing, sash windows, and installed herringbone oak flooring throughout.

The Result

280 sq m of restored living space across four floors. EPC rating improved from F to C. Period character preserved with modern comfort. Completed in 22 weeks with full Building Control sign-off and an insurance-backed warranty on all structural works.

Bespoke Kitchen
Bromley · Sundridge Park

Architectural Kitchen & Dining Renovation

The Challenge

The existing kitchen was a standard builder's fit-out from a previous renovation — flat-pack cabinetry, laminate worktops, and poor lighting. The clients, keen home cooks, wanted a kitchen worthy of entertaining: bespoke joinery, premium materials, and a layout that could handle both daily family life and weekend dinner parties for twelve.

Our Solution

Designed and installed hand-crafted cabinetry painted in deep forest green with brushed brass hardware. Installed Calacatta gold quartz waterfall worktops on the central island, a concealed pocket-door pantry, and integrated Bora downdraft extraction. Added a layered lighting scheme — task lighting under wall units, ambient LED strips in the ceiling coving, and pendant lights over the island — all controlled by a smart home system.

The Result

A fully bespoke kitchen that anchors the ground floor. Completed in 5 weeks with minimal disruption — the family remained in residence throughout, with the site sealed off from the rest of the house each evening.

Loft Conversion
Bromley · Bickley

Hip-to-Gable Double Bedroom & Home Office

The Challenge

An end-of-terrace property with a hipped roof — the sloping side roof meant a standard rear dormer alone wouldn't generate enough usable headroom. The clients needed two rooms, not one: a guest bedroom and a dedicated home office for hybrid working. Both had to feel spacious despite the tight roof geometry.

Our Solution

Combined a hip-to-gable conversion (straightening the sloping side roof to create a vertical wall) with a rear dormer to maximise the internal volume. Split the resulting space into two bedrooms: a guest room with integrated storage and a dedicated executive office with high-speed Cat6 cabling, frameless rooflights for all-day natural light, and built-in acoustic insulation between the two rooms.

The Result

48 sq m of new space accommodating two functional rooms. The office has full-height standing room throughout, and the guest bedroom comfortably fits a double bed with storage. Completed in 9 weeks under Permitted Development.

Luxury Bathroom
Kent · Orpington

Minimalist Spa Wetroom & Micro-cement Suite

The Challenge

A dated family bathroom with a shower-over-bath setup and 1980s tiling. The clients wanted a hotel-grade wetroom — but the existing floor structure wasn't designed for a level-access shower, and the property's timber joists needed assessment before any tanking work could begin.

Our Solution

Assessed and reinforced the floor joists, then installed a Schluter-certified tanking system with a floor-level linear drain. Finished the walls and floor in seamless micro-cement with a warm grey tone, fitted recessed wall niches with integrated backlighting, and installed brushed bronze concealed thermostatic shower controls. Added a frameless glass screen and a wall-hung vanity with integrated LED mirror.

The Result

A fully waterproofed, level-access wetroom with a 100% tanking warranty. The micro-cement finish eliminates grout lines entirely — no mould, no maintenance. Completed in 3 weeks.
