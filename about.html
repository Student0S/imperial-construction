The Imperial Philosophy

Over 8 years of relentless attention to detail.

Founded in 2016 by Director Musa Abdul, Imperial Real Estate Construction was established with a clear mandate: to eliminate the common frustrations homeowners face during property development—uncertain budgets, poor communication, and messy sites.

Having frequently been called in across Central and South London to rectify sub-standard work executed by previous contractors, Musa built Imperial around uncompromising craftsmanship, verified trade credentials, and proactive daily management.

"We treat your home as a private sanctuary, not a construction yard. Every detail, from the structural calculation to the final coat of paint, must withstand scrutiny."

— Musa Abdul, Founder & Managing Director

The Four Pillars

Why clients choose Imperial.

We hold our teams to an exacting institutional standard on every residential build.

01 / CERTAINTY

Fixed-Price Guarantee

We provide comprehensive schedules of work and transparent cost breakdowns before any ground is broken. Unforeseen structural discoveries are priced transparently and agreed in writing beforehand—no unexpected final bills.

02 / PROTECTION

£10M Public Liability & Guarantees

Complete peace of mind. We hold comprehensive £10,000,000 commercial insurance coverage and back all completed loft conversions, extensions, and renovations with a 12 to 24-month structural aftercare warranty.

03 / COMMUNICATION

Dedicated Project Management

You have a direct line to your dedicated site manager and Director Musa Abdul. Weekly progress photo reports, structured staged billing milestones, and responsive answers whenever you need them.

04 / RESPECT

Clean & Considerate Sites

We operate clean, organized, and secure working environments with floor-to-ceiling dust screening, carpet protection, and end-of-day site sweeps to minimise disruption to you and your neighbours.
